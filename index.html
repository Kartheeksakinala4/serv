Digital Forensics

Forensic Disk Acquisition & Analysis

Court-admissible digital evidence collection and deep analysis performed by certified forensic examiners.

Disk Acquisition

Forensically sound imaging of storage media using write-blocked hardware and cryptographic verification at every step.

Media Types HDDs, SSDs, NVMe, USB, SD Cards, RAID Arrays
Methods Write-blocked imaging, bit-for-bit cloning, live acquisition
Verification MD5 + SHA-256 hash validation at source and destination
Chain of Custody Full documentation from seizure to courtroom

Forensic Analysis

Deep examination of acquired images to reconstruct events, recover deleted data, and identify evidence of compromise.

File Recovery Deleted files, formatted partitions, slack space, unallocated sectors
Timeline Filesystem timestamps, registry, event logs, browser history
Malware Identification of backdoors, rootkits, RATs, and persistence mechanisms
Reporting Court-admissible reports with expert witness testimony available
