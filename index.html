Data Recovery

Recovery When It Matters Most

Cutting-edge hardware and software solutions to recover data from the most challenging scenarios.

ISO Class 5 Cleanroom

Laminar Chamber for Physical HDD Recovery

Our ISO-certified laminar flow cleanroom meets Class 5 (ISO 14644-1) standards — the same environment used by hard drive manufacturers. This controlled environment is essential for opening sealed drives, replacing platters, read/write heads, and PCBs without introducing contamination that would permanently destroy data.

  • Head crash and stiction recovery
  • Platter transplant for severely damaged drives
  • Firmware-level repair and sector remapping
  • PCB replacement and component-level repair
  • Demagnetised or overwritten sector recovery
